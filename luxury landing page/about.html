About Us

Luxury Watches is a distinguished establishment in the world of luxury watches, catering to discerning clients who appreciate the finer things in life. With a focus on pre-owned timepieces from renowned brands such as Rolex, Omega, Cartier, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, Tudor, IWC, and many others, we stand as a trusted destination for those seeking quality, style, and authenticity in their horological acquisitions.

At Gentry Timepieces, we understand the unique allure of a well-crafted watch and the stories they carry. Our curated collection reflects our commitment to excellence and our passion for horology. Whether you are looking to add a prestigious timepiece to your collection, trade in your current watch for an upgrade, or consign a piece for a new owner to cherish, our team of watch experts is here to assist you every step of the way.
We take pride in offering a seamless and personalized experience to our clients, providing attentive service and expert guidance to ensure that each transaction is handled with the utmost professionalism and care. With a deep knowledge of the industry and a keen eye for quality, we strive to deliver a level of expertise that sets us apart in the world of luxury watches.

At Gentry Timepieces, we not only sell watches but also cultivate a community of watch enthusiasts who share our love for craftsmanship and heritage. Whether you are a seasoned collector looking for a rare gem or a first-time buyer seeking a timeless classic, Gentry Timepieces welcomes you to explore our exquisite selection and discover the artistry and heritage of luxury watches. Join us on a journey of horological excellence, where every tick of the clock tells a story of elegance, precision, and timeless sophistication.
